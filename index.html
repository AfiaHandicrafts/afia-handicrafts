Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Are your products handmade?
Yes — every piece is hand-forged by skilled artisans, so slight variations are natural and part of the craft.

Q: Do you offer customization?
Yes, we accept custom size, finish, and design requests. Contact us with your requirements.

Q: How do I care for metal products?
Wipe with a dry soft cloth. Avoid harsh chemicals or prolonged moisture exposure to preserve the finish.

Q: What are the payment options?
Advance payment for custom/bulk orders, with Cash on Delivery (COD) available in most areas for regular orders.

Shipping, COD & Returns

Shipping: Orders are shipped via courier within a few days of confirmation. Delivery timelines vary by location and customization.

Cash on Delivery: Available in most serviceable areas. An advance payment may be required for custom or bulk orders.

Returns: Please inspect your order on delivery. For damaged or incorrect items, contact us within 48 hours with photos for resolution.
